Privacy Policy

Alfred · Effective October 27, 2026 · Updated October 27, 2026

Alfred is a private voice assistant that runs on its owner's personal laptop. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google accounts. This policy explains what Google user data Alfred accesses and how it is handled.

Data Alfred accesses

  • Google Calendar: event titles, times, locations, guests and descriptions, to answer questions such as "What's on today?", and to create, change or delete events when the owner asks. Deleting an event, or any change that notifies other people, happens only after the owner confirms aloud.
  • Google Tasks: task lists and tasks, to read them aloud and to add, change, complete or delete tasks when asked. Deleting a task happens only after the owner confirms aloud.
  • Gmail: the ability to create, change, delete and send drafts written for the owner. A draft is sent only after the owner explicitly confirms aloud. Alfred does not read the owner's inbox or received email.
  • Google Contacts: names and email addresses of saved contacts and of people the owner has emailed before, to address drafts.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out the owner's spoken requests. When the owner asks a question, the relevant information (for example, today's events) is sent to Anthropic's Claude API so that Alfred can compose a spoken reply. Anthropic processes this data to produce the reply, under Anthropic's commercial terms and privacy policy. No other third party receives it.

Storage

Sign-in tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's laptop. Alfred does not keep a copy of calendar entries, tasks, contacts or emails beyond the current conversation, except in a local log file on the owner's laptop. Nothing is stored on any server operated by Alfred's developer.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, used for advertising, shared with data brokers, or used to train artificial intelligence models. Alfred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

The owner can revoke Alfred's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the tokens and log files from the laptop.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
