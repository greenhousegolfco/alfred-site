Alfred

A private voice assistant for one household.

Alfred is a voice assistant that runs on its owner's personal Windows laptop. Its owner speaks to it to check the weather, hear their schedule, manage their to-do list, draft emails, and hand work to software tools.

Who it is for

Alfred is not a public product. It is used only by the person who built it, with their own Google accounts. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.

What it connects to

  • Google Calendar, to read the owner's schedule and to create, change or delete events when asked.
  • Google Tasks, to read, add, change, complete or delete the owner's tasks when asked.
  • Gmail, to create email drafts for the owner, which are sent only after the owner confirms aloud.
  • Google Contacts (including people the owner has emailed), to find email addresses for the owner's drafts.

How Alfred handles this information is described in the privacy policy.
